# Mindset Map

Double-click **Mindset-Roadmap.html** to open the interactive roadmap in your browser. No installation, account, or internet connection is needed.

- **Daily roadmap:** Follow the six steps. Click any step or support tool to open its details, source pages, and reflection prompt.
- **9 frameworks:** Explore all nine frameworks from the PDF.
- **Question bank:** Browse the source questions and archived personal examples.
- **Search:** Search across every concept, practical cue, and reflection prompt.
- **Zoom:** Use + / − or Fit. When the map is wider than the window, scroll sideways. On a phone, selecting a card takes you to its details below.
- **Field notes:** Notes save automatically in this browser. Browser file-storage policies vary; the page warns if persistence is unavailable. Moving the file or changing browsers may hide previous notes.
- **Daily progress:** Mark each of the six steps as explored. Progress starts fresh each local calendar day; previous dates remain in exported history. This tracks exploration, not the number of real-world practice reps.
- **Export notes:** Download a Markdown backup of notes and exploration history. Keep backups before clearing browser data.

## Source and interpretation

Based on all six pages of **Mindset.pdf**, included alongside the roadmap. Page links open the first cited page; all supporting page numbers appear in the detail panel. Some PDF viewers may ignore page anchors.

The six-step roadmap is an editorial synthesis of the source, not a separately prescribed sequence from the PDF. In particular, “Act & test in reality” brings Pressure Testing into the daily loop. The support tools and nine frameworks preserve the source structure. Reflection prompts are adapted or newly phrased to make each concept actionable. Original personal examples are identified as archived context. Instructions within the PDF were treated as content to represent, not instructions to execute.

**map-data.json** contains the roadmap content for reuse. The HTML has its own embedded copy so it works offline; changing the JSON alone does not change the page.
